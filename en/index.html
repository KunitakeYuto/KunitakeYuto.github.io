Doctoral Student, Degree Programs in Systems and Information Engineering, Graduate School of Science and Technology, University of Tsukuba
Part-time Lecturer, School of Management and Information Sciences, Tama University
Part-time Lecturer, Tsukuba Institute of Science and Technology
National Institute of Advanced Industrial Science and Technology (AIST)

I study consumer protection in the digital domain. My main topics are competition and consumer policy for the metaverse, user-generated content (UGC) markets, and the creator economy, as well as the governance of social VR. Comments on my papers and inquiries about joint research are welcome.
